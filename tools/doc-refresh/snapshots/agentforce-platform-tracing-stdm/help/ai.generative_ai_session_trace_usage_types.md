Billing Considerations for Agentforce Session Tracing

Agentforce Session Tracing consumes Data 360 credits for storage used over the allocated amount.

REQUIRED EDITIONS
Available in: Enterprise, Performance, and Unlimited Editions with an Einstein for Sales, Einstein for Platform, Einstein for Service, Einstein 1 Service, or Einstein GPT Service add-on. To purchase add-ons, contact your Salesforce account executive.
TIP

This feature has access to Digital Wallet, a free account management tool that offers near real-time consumption data for enabled products across your active contracts. Access Digital Wallet and start tracking your org's usage. To learn more, see About Digital Wallet.

NOTE Agentforce Session Tracing no longer consumes Flex or Data 360 Data Services credits. Storage consumed by Agentforce Observability entities are associated with the Data 360 Storage Beyond Allocation usage type and are included in Data Storage Allocation in Digital Wallet.
DIGITAL WALLET CARD USAGE TYPE DESCRIPTION
Data Storage Storage Beyond Allocation Usage is calculated based on the amount of storage used above the amount allocated.

For more information on Data 360 usage billing, refer to your contract or contact your account executive.
